CHAPTER TWO

Denzel was expelled two days before the school's closing day.Mind you denzel's father had been called to school the previous day.He was called to school together with other parents.They were told that their children had failed to reach the school's passmark in the end of term exams and hence they could not move on to the other grade they had to repeat.Less than twenty four hours after denzel's father had just been told that denzel would not be moving on to the next grade he was called to come back to school the following day and that was when he was told that denzel had been expelled.They picked his things from school and they went home.Denzel did not actually have many things as most of the things that he had while joining the boarding school had been stolen.Denzel's father was so disappointed with him.He even told him that he would not find a new school for him since this was the third time in two years that he had been expelled.That was how denzel quitted school.The reason why he was so violent was because his neighbourhood was the headquarters of all street fights.They even had a tournament for street fights.Since he was now idle he used to go and watch the street fights.They even had an arena where all the street fights were held.One day one of the fighters who was to take part in the tournament got sick and he could not fight.The organisers of the tournament needed a fighter since so much money had been spent to organise the tournament hence they could not cancel it.Denzel volunteered to participate in the tournament.It was his first street fight.If he won the fight he was to be given a hundred dollars.He had hope since he was fighting an amateur who was also fighting his first street fight.Their fight was just a curtain raiser before the professional boxers entered the ring.Well the fight did not end up well for him since the fight ended even before it started.In the first minute of the fight denzel received a punch that sent him straight to the floor.Despite the fact that denzel was a body builder the person he was fighting was not a pro but was a construction worker hence his hands were naturally rough and tough.After the knock out punch denzel passed out and the next thing he saw was himself in a hospital bed.He woke up and saw a nurse next to him who was asking him how he was feeling but by that time he had already lost six teeth so he could barely speak properly.Denzel's father thinking that he had seen enough from denzel was given another call this time round from the hospital.At this point he was fed up.They went to the hospital together with denzel's brothers.Denzel had two brothers william and tash,they lived together with their father since their mother died when they were young.Denzel's father had always suspected that denzel was not his child.This came out of many factors.One of them being that out of the three denzel was the naughtiest.Also denzel never resembled him.While they were in the hospital he decided to do a secret D.N.A test on all his sons.Denzel was discharged from the hospital and they went back home.After seventy two hours the D.N.A results came out.Denzel's father could not believe his eyes the results claimed that denzel was his son while william and tash were not.He could not believe that the one who gave him endless stress was his son and the rest were not.Denzel's father felt betrayed by the D.N.A results.He was so disappointed with his dead wife for lying to him all those years that they were together.And also for cheating on him twice as william and tash were three years appart.That was the moment that changed their family forever.Denzel's father had just lost his car and land which had been repossessed by the bank after he had failed to pay his loan.Mind you he had taken that loan so that he would pay university school fees for tash and william.He had sacrificed his whole life to take care of tash and william only to find out they were actually not his sons.The big question that he kept on asking himself was who was their father.Everything came to light during one of the burrials that denzel's father had attended.It was the burrial of his childhood friend melvin.Melvin had given instructions that during his burial people should not cry instead they should organise a great feast and drink alcohol and they should party in memory of him since he was a drunkard.Since he loved beer everyone who attended the burial was given a bottle of beer as they entered the burial.It was more of a celebration.That was when denzel's father met victoria.Victoria was the best friend to denzel's mother.Victoria was also a close friend to melvin.Denzel's father knew that victoria was the only person on earth who knew all of her wife's deepest secrets.Victoria was also a drunkard.Denzel's father waited till victoria was totally drunk since he knew victoria would only be truthful when she was not sober.He went where she was to give her company.Victoria was so excited to meet him after so many years.
