CHAPTER TWO

Speaking of bad days if you thought that jimmy was having a bad day then that was just a fraction of what victor was going through.Victor had been searching for a job for the longest time .His job was actually looking for a job as he would wake up every day get dressed and go from one office to another with his c.v. hoping one of the companies would notice him.Finally his prayers were answered as he received an invitation for an interview.He could not even sleep on that day .He spent the whole day preparing.He picked out his favourite suit which he inherited from his grandfather as the suit of luck.He washed it and hunged it so that he could wear it the following day.He woke up early in the morning and took a shower and he went to the hanging line to collect his suit.To his surprise his suit had been stolen.The thief stole the suit and left only the pegs.Since he had no time he decided to go to the market and buy another suit as fast as possible.When he arrived in the market he entered one of the shops and he was surprised .He found his suit being sold in the shop.It was barely wet the thief did not even wait for it to dry up.Victor could even identify one of the marks that the suit had as when he was ironing it it got slightly burnt.In as much as victor wanted to put up a fight he did not have the time as he had to get ready for the big day ahead as he had a job interview to attend to.He could not believe that he was buying back his stolen lucky suit.The seller was even selling it at a high price.The seller was shamelessly telling him that this was the latest fashion that he had just imported from europe.While victor knew that this was the same suit his grandfather wore fifty years ago in his wedding.Victor bought it ,indeed he had been welcomed in town.Victor decided to rush home and get his papers as he still had some few hours to the interview.He went to the bus stage so that he would board a bus home.There was a bus already at the stage but victor refused to board it as it was empty and victor had no time to wait for it to be full.Instead he boarded another one which seemed to be old but it was almost full and it was charging fare at half the normal price.Victor boarded it as it was cheap forgetting that when the deal is too good think twice.Just as the old bus hit the road the weather changed and it started raining.The old bus had holes at the top hence the rain water was pouring into the bus and getting to where they were sitted.It got worse and victor had to buy an umbrella.Victor could not beleive that he was inside a bus buy he still needed to use an umbrella.It did not take long before the bus broke down.They had to get down and try to push it so that it may move.Just when victor alighted the old bus to push it the bus he had refused to board passed them.The driver was actually so cruel as he passed so fast and ended up spilling rain water that was on the road on them.Mind you victor was wearing a white suit.After pushing it for half an hour they gave up.At that time victor was smelling like smoke as the bus was only emitting smoke and not moving.Victor decided to run home as it was not even that far as he did not have time on his side.In the middle of the running he accidentally stepped on a banana pill and he slipped and fell on the muddy floor.Not forgetting he was still wearing the white suit.At this point he was conviced that his village people were doing everything to stop him from attending the job interview.Despite all the challlenges victor managed to prepare himself and he attended the interview.Upon arrival he met a huge crowd lining up at the gate of the company.At first he thought that the company had so many wokers that they had to line up while checking in for work.He tried to pass the line and that was when he met the watchman who was actually a pregnant woman and she did not spare him for trying to pass the line.

watchwoman:young man so you think that the people in the line are chilling at the beach.You think you are so special to skip the line go to the far back or go home we have no time for your nonsense.

Speaking of a bad day, the day was about to be a long one as the female watchman did not spare victor with insults.It is one thing to be insulted and it is another to be insulted infront of everyone and you can do nothing about it as you want the job so bad.The line was so huge as seven hundred people had come out for the job interview while they only needed the best ten.Victor who only had a degree qualification was surprised to find himself in the same line with people who had masters and there were two people who had a P.H.D.,Victor had no work experience only to find himself in the line with someone who had been in that field for twenty years.There was so much tension in the line .The weather was chilly and cold but victor was sweating you would have thought that he was just from the shower.He even tried to eat some food to calm the tension but the food had become tasteless.No one was even talking to the other in the line .The policy was every man for himself God for us all.
