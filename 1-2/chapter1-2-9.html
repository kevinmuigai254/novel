CHAPTER NINE

As martin was living his dream life catherine on the other hand was going through a process of acceptance and healing.She was going to therapy and counseling where she learnt to accept her condition.She realised that she was going through all this since she always entered into relationships to escape from poverty but this time round she decided to take charge of her life.She decided that she would no longer wait for a man to come and provide for her instead she decided to look for a job.She went and made her C.V. and she always had a copy of her C.V in her handbag everywhere she went.She was desperately searching for a job everytime she went to church she would give random people her C.V .At one point she even went to a funeral and when the funeral ended she began giving random people her C.V to her luck it landed on a very senior person in one of the biggest companies in the country.Coincidentally the company was hiring hence catherine was invited for the job interview.She prepared herself properly for the interview.On the day of the interview she woke up very early in the morning and rushed to the interview venue.She arrived at the venue at four am thinking that she would be the first person only for her to find a very long queue of other people who had attended the interview.Some of them arrived at midnight and they spent the whole night there despite the fact that the interview was starting at ten o'clock.The interview began.One of the people in the line was chased away as he was drunk.It was ironical as the company they were interviewing at was an alcohol making and selling company.The man thought he would get the job by proving that he was a customer of the company's products.After an hour they were told to move to the field as the number of people participating in the interview was huge.More than five hundred were present and yet the company only needed to hire five people.Finally after waiting for five hours in the line she was next.She entered the interview room where she met a panel which was to interview her.Catherine had spent the previous three days going through all videos online showing how to behave in an interview hence she was ready.She was asked to briefly describe herself.As soon as she mentioned the highschool that she studied at the H.R. manager,lucy, who was seated in the panel recognised her.They were in the same highschool but catherine was in a higher grade than her.At one point they were bedmates.The school beds were double deckers and lucy used to sleep on the upper bed while catherine slept on the lower bed.One day lucy had taken alot of water and when they went to sleep she wet the bed.Since she was on the upper bed the urine went down to catherine's bed.Catherine had a habit of sleeping while opening her mouth,so the urine went straight into her mouth.When catherine woke up she found urine all over her bed.On that day she gave lucy a record beating.After that she forced her to wash a muddy tree with a handkerchief ,soap and a bucket of water.That day was a nightmare for lucy.From that day catherine started bullying lucy in school.Now twenty years later they had now met each other,catherine asking for a job and lucy as the H.R. manager.That is how catherine lost that opportunity to be employed in the biggest alcohol manufacturing company in the country.After failing to get that job she did not give up she still continued looking for a job.She would go round in all companies and businesses in the city asking if they had a job vacancy.After going round she found a business which had a job vacancy.It was a mortuary.Since she desparately needed a job she took the mortuary job.The qualification to being given the job was that you needed to be free from diseases like high blood pressure and heart attacks as it was not an easy job.Catherine thought that her job was to look after the dead bodies so that they may not be stolen but when she got hired she found out that the job was more complicated.Her job was to receive dead bodies,wash them and after that she would put clothes on them,the clothes were brought by family members of the dead.There were two shifts in the job the day time shift and the night shift.Nobody ever wanted to be in the nightshift but since catherine was new in the job she was assigned to the night shift.She was very energetic in her new job and she did it with passion as she now had a source of income which she could use to take care of her three children.Untill one day as she was cleaning one of the dead bodies in the middle of the night the lights went out.Her body became frozen at that moment she did not know what to do.At that moment one of the caskets that was not kept well fell and it opened up.When the lights went back on catherine found a dead body on the floor.That was the moment that she ran away and never looked back. With the speed at which she was moving at escaping from the mortuary if she had been participating in the olympics she would have won a medal.She never ever returned to the mortuary again.
