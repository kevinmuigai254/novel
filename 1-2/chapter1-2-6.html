CHAPTER SIX

After days of waiting for peter to get out of the coma he finally died.It was very heart breaking for martin and the rest of the cartel members as they had risked their lives in the mission and in return they were to get out with nothing.This was not the only moment that their mission ended up in a failure and they lost the money.Three years ago martin together with peter went on a similar mission.Peter was the closest person to peter in the cartel movement,he was actually his best friend.This time round they were returning back from a successful mission.They were in the car and they had money in the bag.It was late in the night.By this time martin was not married and peter was also single.Since they had the money they decided to go and celebrate their success in the club.They were now the latest millionaires in town.Inside the club as they were drinking and dancing they spotted two girls who were alone.Martin picked one and peter picked the other one.The next thing that happened is that they ended up booking two rooms.Martin together with his girl took one of the rooms and peter together with his girl took the other.After the long night of enjoying themselves martin was woken up by the hotel cleaners who had come to clean the room.He looked at his watch and it was ten o'clock in the morning.He could not find the lady who he was with the whole night.The lady was not the only thing missing,everything was gone.Martin was left naked all the clothes that he had were stolen.He had to wear the bathrobe which he found in the shower of the room.He went to the other room and the situation was the same for peter everything that he had had been stolen by the ladies.To make the matter worse they found that both their car and the money had also been stolen.Martin could not believe that the previous night he slept as a millionaire only to wake up broke with nothing.The death of peter really broke martin down,martin decided to go back to church.During that church service martin was seated at the front of the church.The pastor spotted him during his preaching.That was the moment that the pastor decided to prophesy over martin's life.He prophesied that that year was the year of prosperity for martin.He prophesied that martin would be the next jackport winner.When martin heard that he was so excited as soon the church service ended he went straight home and took all the money that he had left and placed a bet at the lottery since the pastor had prophesied that he would be the next jackport winner.He had placed a bet on twenty games and if he won the bet he would be given two hundred million dollars as the mega jackport winner.He watched all the games one by one confirming his bet was on.That was double the money he was promised by the cartel movement.All nineteen games went exactly as he had predicted and only one game was remaining.The rules were you had to predict correctly all the twenty games to win the money and if even one went wrong you would lose your money.This final game was the only thing separating him from the two million dollars.Martin was trembling while watching the game.He had predicted that the game would end one nil.At half time the score was one nil and his prediction was intact but he still had another forty five minutes of waiting for the game to end.That was the longest forty five minutes of his life.Finally the game reached ninety minutes and the referee added one more minute.Martin was one minute away from winning two hundred million dollars.In the last minute of the game the opponent team got a penalty and they scored and the game ended one one.That is how martin lost his chance of winning two hundred million dollars.He got so mad that he ended up destroying the television.From that day he hated the pastor who had prophesied that he would win the lottery,he hated that man with passion as he made him use all the money he had on the bet.Martin also hated the church and he decided to be an atheist.He even attended one of the atheist events.It was an annual event where all ateists in the country came together.The event took place in a very luxurious venue where everything was golden including the gate opening up to the venue.Martin went to the parking lot and all supercars were present.Very rich and prominent people had attended the event.Martin had never seen anything like this in his entire life.When he attended the event he was the only new person in the event as the others knew each other.They even did a welcoming ceremony for him to welcome him to the group of atheists.He was given an opportunity to address them as they really valued visitors.The unexpected happened,as soon as martin was given the microphone to address them the crowd went silent and they all paid attention to martin.Martin was so nervous as it was his first time in such a meeting.He ended up tripping and on his way down he shouted Jesus.The microphone was on so everyone heard him shout Jesus and that is how he got kicked out of the atheist event.Mind you he got kicked out when they were just about to start eating their special meal.
