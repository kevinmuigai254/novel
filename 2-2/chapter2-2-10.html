Emma on the other hand decided to go back to school.Her parent's did a second fundraiser and they got money for school fees.After getting the schoolfees money she returned back to the university.This time round she had learnt her lesson.This time round she had no time for parties or even relationships.She used to spend most of her time in the library studying to the extent that some new students thought that she was the librarian since she was always in the library.She used to study so hard to the extenet that if a student wanted to know if a certain book was in the library they would first consult emma since she had been through all the books in the library.She was not only studying hard but she was also perfoming very well to the extent that she became the lecturer's most favourite student.After five years emma graduated with first class honours.This time round her family was naive since the last time they attended her graduation they found out she was still in the first year.Her parents used to visit her regularly to ensure she was really studying and not wasting their schoolfees.She graduated and her parents did a very big celebration to honour her.Now the problem was not graduating the challenge was getting a job as the level of joblessness in the country was at a record level.She really struggled to get a job.She moved from one company to another asking if they had a job opportunity.One day after spending the whole day searching for a job she saw a young man selling bananas by the road side.Since she was hungry she decided to go and buy the bananas.She told the guy selling the bananas that she had been looking for a job the whole day.The guy asked her what course she had done.That was when emma told him that she had a degree in journalism.The guy selling bananas told him that he also studied journalism and he actually had a masters but due to unemployment he ended up selling bananas at the roadside to earn a living.Emma had never been heartbroken that much.Finally after weeks of searching for a job she got employed in a supermarket.Well she used to attend the same church which the owner of the supermarket used to attend.So during one of the sunday services she was the one leading the church in prayers and at that point she announced that she was jobless and she pleaded with them to employ her if they had a job opportunity.Luckily the owner of the supermarket had attended the church on that day and that is how she got the job.Even if it was not her dream job to work at the supermarket it was a good starting point since she needed an income.She started working at the supermarket.It was not an easy job since it was among the busiest supermarkets in the city.She was required to be at the work place by six in the morning and they would leave at nine in the evening.She had a really toxic manager who was in charge.The manager never allowed them to sit down they had to stand the whole day and serve the customers.They were also not allowed to use their mobile phones.This job was so toxic that emma started looking for another job even as she was doing this job.One day as she was watching the television an announcement came up.It was the announcement that would change her life forever.The announcement was from one of the largest media stations in the country.It was announced that justin the presenter had just died.Justin was the longest serving presenter in the country as when emma joined school at age four justin was still a presenter.Now that he had died the media company urged people to apply for the job interview.When emma looked at the requirements that they needed she got hope as she had them.She started preparing herself for the interview.She went and asked for a day off at her supermarket job as she lied that her parents were very sick and she needed to go and see them back in the village.This was the opportunity that she had been looking for in months.She attended the interview full of hope.She met hundreds of people who had also attended the interview.Surprisingly among the hundreds of people who had attended the interview was her manager at the supermarket job.The supermarket job was so toxic that even her own boss was looking for another job.Luckily emma was selected among the three people that the company needed to hire.It was a dream come true for her to be in the media.The first time she went live on air her whole family including her father who never watched news began watching the television so that they would see emma.After one year at the job she started earning a six figure salary.She built her parents a very luxurious mansion.She also bought her first car.Since emma was a grateful humanbeing she opened a business for her aunt lily as a way of thanking her since if she had not hosted her at her home emma would never have visited the city.It was a season of blessings for emma and her family as they lived happily ever after.
