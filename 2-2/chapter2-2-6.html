was bad untill another young man called davis picked up the microphone and he decided to share his story.Davis had a girlfriend named bianca.Bianca moved in to his place after one month of dating.One day he was just chilling in a club.He was watching a world cup match from the club.The club was full to the brim as they all turned up to support their country.The atmosphere was electric.Davis had placed a bet on the game.Unfortunately his team lost hence he lost the money.At that time his neighbour called him and told him to tell his girl to leave behind his gas cooker as his girlfriend was bianca leading the demonstrations.She was even interviewed by one of the journalists who were covering the demonstration as she explained the challenges that they go through living with hiv and
